# Requirement Decisions

This document lists business decisions that cannot be finalized from the current Business Requirements Document (`docs/BRD.md`) without additional confirmation.

These items are **not** confirmed requirements. Recommended defaults may be used for later design phases only until a decision owner confirms otherwise.

| Decision ID | Topic | Question | Why It Matters | Recommended Default | Alternatives | Impact |
|-------------|-------|----------|----------------|---------------------|-------------|--------|
| DEC-001 | Commercial pricing model | How will the product itself be priced and packaged for customers (for example subscription tiers, usage, or per-organization licensing)? | Packaging affects which capabilities are included, expected organization size, and commercial constraints. | Defer commercial packaging. Treat the initial product as a single full-capability offering for SMB inventory operations. | Tiered plans; per-warehouse pricing; per-user pricing; usage-based AI pricing. | Affects sales, onboarding, feature flags, and possibly AI usage limits. |
| DEC-002 | Organization scale targets | What is the expected maximum size of an organization in the initial product (users, warehouses, products, transactions)? | Scale expectations affect process design, reporting usefulness, and later technical capacity planning. | Design for typical SMB scale: tens of users, a small number of warehouses, thousands of products, and ongoing daily purchasing and sales activity. | Micro-business only; large multi-branch enterprise from day one. | Affects usability, reporting, and later performance/scalability work. |
| DEC-003 | Warehouse permission model | Should inventory, receiving, transfers, and warehouse reporting be restricted to assigned warehouses, or are organization roles sufficient? | Warehouse isolation changes staffing models and reduces accidental cross-warehouse activity. | Organization roles apply globally. Optionally, an organization may restrict INVENTORY_STAFF (and similar operational roles) to assigned warehouses. ADMIN and MANAGER may access all warehouses in their organization unless the organization chooses otherwise. | No warehouse restrictions; strict warehouse-only access for all non-admin roles; separate warehouse-manager role. | Affects user management, operational workflows, reporting, AI answers, and audit interpretation. |
| DEC-004 | Product variants | Are size/color/style variants required as first-class catalog entities, or is each sellable item a distinct product/SKU? | Variants change catalog design, inventory identity, purchasing, and sales. | Initial product: one SKU = one inventory item. No variant matrix. | Variant parent with child SKUs; optional attributes without inventory split. | Affects catalog, inventory, purchasing, sales, and AI recommendations. |
| DEC-005 | Unit conversion | How complex should unit-of-measure conversion be (for example purchase in cases, stock in eaches)? | Conversion errors directly affect inventory accuracy and purchasing. | Each product has one stocking unit. Optional simple conversion to a purchase or sales unit using a single numeric factor. No multi-step or dimensional conversion in the initial product. | Stocking unit only, no conversion; full conversion tables; packing hierarchies. | Affects receiving, sales quantities, reporting, and AI quantity suggestions. |
| DEC-006 | Inventory valuation method | Which method should be used to value inventory (weighted average, FIFO, standard cost, or another method)? | Valuation affects inventory value reports, profitability views, and management decisions. | Use a single organization-selectable method, with **weighted average cost** as the recommended default. Do not support simultaneous methods for the same organization without a later decision. | FIFO; standard cost; last cost; valuation deferred to accounting systems. | Affects receiving, adjustments, returns, reports, and AI explanations of value. |
| DEC-007 | Currency | Will organizations operate in one currency or multiple currencies? | Currency affects prices, purchase orders, sales, payments, and reports. | One base currency per organization. All operational amounts are recorded in that currency. | Multi-currency documents with conversion; per-warehouse currency. | Affects catalog pricing, purchasing, sales, payments, reporting, and AI numeric statements. |
| DEC-008 | Tax handling | How should tax be treated on purchases and sales? | Tax can pull the product toward accounting/compliance scope. | Record optional tax amounts on sales and purchase documents as commercial fields. Do not provide tax determination, filing, or statutory compliance. | No tax fields; jurisdiction-specific tax engines; full tax compliance. | Affects document totals, invoices, and whether the product remains outside full accounting. |
| DEC-009 | Purchase approval thresholds | Which purchase orders require approval, and who may approve them? | Approval rules affect procurement speed, control, and AI purchase drafts. | All non-draft purchase orders require approval by ADMIN or MANAGER before they become Approved. No monetary threshold in the initial product. | Threshold by amount; threshold by supplier; role-based amount limits; no approval for small orders. | Affects purchasing workflow, notifications, audit, and AI-assisted PO actions. |
| DEC-010 | AI action approval thresholds | Which AI-proposed actions require explicit human approval? | This is the boundary between assistance and unauthorized operational change. | All high-impact AI write actions require explicit human approval: purchase order creation/submission, inventory adjustments, stock transfers, cancellations of committed documents, and price changes. Read-only answers do not. | Amount-based thresholds; auto-execute low-risk drafts; manager-only approval. | Affects AI design, audit, operational risk, and user trust. |
| DEC-011 | Data retention | How long must operational, audit, and AI records be retained? | Retention affects compliance, storage, audit usefulness, and privacy. | Retain operational and audit history for the life of the organization unless a later retention policy is defined. AI conversation content may have a shorter retention policy after confirmation. | Fixed year-based retention; legal-hold exceptions; separate audit retention. | Affects audit, reporting, privacy, and later storage design. |
| DEC-012 | SUPER_ADMIN access to tenant data | May a platform SUPER_ADMIN view or change an organization's operational inventory, sales, and purchase data? | This is a trust, privacy, and multi-tenant isolation question. | SUPER_ADMIN may manage platform users, organization lifecycle, and system configuration. Access to tenant operational records is not a normal duty and, if ever allowed for support, must be exceptional, authorized, and audited. | Full operational access; support impersonation; no tenant data access at all. | Affects security, support processes, audit, and customer trust. |
| DEC-013 | Multi-organization users | May one person belong to more than one organization? | Membership rules affect login, permissions, and isolation. | A business user belongs to one organization. Platform SUPER_ADMIN is not an organization operating user. | Multi-organization membership with explicit org switching; consultant access across tenants. | Affects user management, authentication experience, and isolation rules. |
| DEC-014 | Damaged and expired stock | Are damaged and expired quantities part of Stock on Hand, or held in a separate non-sellable bucket? | The required formula is Available Stock = Stock on Hand − Reserved Stock. Extra buckets change that meaning. | Damaged and expired events reduce sellable Stock on Hand through traceable movements. If the business still physically holds the goods, they may be recorded as non-sellable quantity that is not available to promise. Any additional formula beyond the required Available Stock definition must be confirmed. | Keep damaged/expired inside Stock on Hand but excluded from Available Stock by changing the formula; immediate write-off only. | Affects inventory meaning, valuation, warehouse processes, reports, and AI answers. |
| DEC-015 | Partial sales fulfillment | Can a sales order be shipped or completed in parts? | Partial fulfillment changes reservation, deduction, and order state. | Allow partial fulfillment: remaining reserved quantity stays committed until completed or cancelled. | Complete-only fulfillment; backorders as separate orders. | Affects sales lifecycle, inventory reservation, reporting, and notifications. |
| DEC-016 | Invoicing depth | Are invoices operational sales documents, or a step toward accounts receivable accounting? | Invoice scope can expand the product into accounting. | Sales invoices are commercial records of a sale for operational history and payment recording. They are not a general ledger, accounts receivable subledger, or tax filing system. | No invoices; full AR/AP accounting; statutory invoicing. | Affects sales close, payments, reports, and out-of-scope accounting boundary. |
| DEC-017 | Payment recording depth | How completely should payments against sales and purchases be recorded? | Payments can expand into banking and accounting. | Allow recording of payment status and amounts against sales and purchase documents (for example unpaid, partially paid, paid). No bank feeds, cash management, or reconciliation. | Status only; no payments; full payment allocation and banking. | Affects customer/supplier history and keeps accounting out of scope. |
| DEC-018 | Lot, batch, and serial tracking | Is lot/batch/serial tracking required to support expiry and traceability? | Expiry, recalls, and some industries require identity below SKU. | Optional lot or batch and expiry date at receiving and stock movement where the organization enables it. Serial-number tracking is future scope. | No lots; mandatory lots; full serialization. | Affects receiving, sales, returns, expiry, transfers, and AI stock answers. |
| DEC-019 | Sales approval | Do sales orders require approval before they reserve stock? | Approval changes speed of fulfillment and control of discounts or credit. | Sales staff may confirm standard sales orders without a separate approval step. Cancellation of confirmed orders and unusual overrides may require MANAGER or ADMIN. | All sales require approval; approval above a value threshold. | Affects sales workflow, reservations, and audit. |
| DEC-020 | Transfer approval | Do warehouse transfers require approval? | Transfers move value between locations and can hide stock. | Transfers between warehouses require MANAGER or ADMIN approval before dispatch, except that INVENTORY_STAFF may draft them. | No approval; approval only above quantity/value; warehouse-manager approval. | Affects transfer workflow, notifications, AI transfer drafts, and audit. |
| DEC-021 | Negative stock policy | May the organization allow stock on hand to go negative? | Negative stock destroys inventory trust and fulfillment reliability. | Do not allow stock on hand or available stock to become negative. Sales cannot confirm more than available stock unless a later decision allows backorders. | Allow backorders; allow negative SOH with warning. | Affects sales, transfers, adjustments, concurrency expectations, and AI actions. |
| DEC-022 | Backorders | If requested quantity exceeds available stock, may the order be accepted as a backorder? | Backorders change reservation, fulfillment, and customer promises. | No backorders in the initial product. Users must reduce quantity, split demand, or wait for stock. | Allow backorders; allow reserve-what-is-available and backorder the rest. | Affects sales, notifications, and inventory availability meaning. |
| DEC-023 | Cost visibility by role | Which roles may see cost, supplier prices, and inventory value? | Cost is commercially sensitive. | ADMIN and MANAGER may see cost and inventory value. SALES_STAFF see selling prices, not cost, unless granted. VIEWER sees operational quantities; cost visibility is configurable and defaults off. INVENTORY_STAFF see quantities, not cost, by default. | All roles see cost; cost only for ADMIN. | Affects reporting, AI answers, and permission design. |
| DEC-024 | AI data freshness | How current must AI answers be relative to live inventory? | Stale AI answers can cause bad purchasing or fulfillment decisions. | AI must use the same authorized business information available to the user and must indicate if information may be stale. Exact freshness windows are a later operational decision. | Always live; hourly snapshots; daily briefing only. | Affects trust, notifications, and AI architecture later. |
| DEC-025 | Localization | Which languages and regional formats must the initial product support? | Language and formats affect SMB adoption. | Initial product: one primary language (English) with organization timezone and locale for dates and numbers. Additional languages are future scope. | Multi-language at launch; language per user. | Affects UI, notifications, and AI responses. |
| DEC-026 | Opening stock source | How is opening stock established for an organization going live? | Opening stock is the foundation of inventory accuracy. | Authorized users enter opening stock per product and warehouse with reason and audit history. Optional import of opening balances is expected as a business capability; exact import format is later design. | Opening stock only via purchase receipts; no opening stock. | Affects onboarding, inventory ledger, valuation, and audit. |
| DEC-027 | Customer and supplier uniqueness | How are duplicate customer or supplier records prevented? | Duplicates damage history, performance, and AI recommendations. | Require unique supplier and customer codes within an organization. Name uniqueness is encouraged but not assumed perfect. Duplicate-merge is future scope. | Unique names; fuzzy match warnings; no uniqueness. | Affects master data quality, reports, and AI supplier advice. |
| DEC-028 | Reserved stock sources | Which documents reserve stock? | Reservation changes Available Stock. | Confirmed sales orders reserve stock. Draft sales do not. Transfers may reserve at the source after approval and before dispatch if needed so the same stock is not sold. Purchase orders do not increase available stock before receipt. | Reserve at draft; no transfer reservation; reserve on invoice only. | Affects availability, sales, transfers, and AI stock answers. |
| DEC-029 | Purchase return inventory timing | When does a supplier return reduce stock? | Timing affects stock on hand during return disputes. | Stock decreases when the supplier return is confirmed/dispatched, through a purchase-return movement. Draft returns do not change stock. | Decrease only after supplier acknowledgement; quarantine first. | Affects inventory, purchasing, and audit. |
| DEC-030 | Customer return restock | When does a customer return increase sellable stock? | Restocking unsellable goods creates false availability. | Returned goods increase sellable stock only after inspection confirms they are sellable. Damaged returns follow damaged-stock handling. | Always restock immediately; never auto-restock. | Affects inventory, returns, valuation, and available stock. |
| DEC-031 | Reorder point ownership | Are reorder points user-maintained, AI-suggested, or both? | This changes purchasing behavior and accountability. | Users maintain reorder levels and low-stock thresholds. AI may recommend changes but must not silently overwrite them. | AI-managed min/max only; no user thresholds. | Affects catalog, notifications, purchasing, and AI recommendations. |
| DEC-032 | Organization self-registration | Can a business create its own organization, or is organization creation performed by a platform administrator? | This affects onboarding and SUPER_ADMIN duties. | A qualified user may create an organization during onboarding and become its initial ADMIN. Platform SUPER_ADMIN may also create organizations. | SUPER_ADMIN-only creation; invite-only. | Affects onboarding workflow and security. |
| DEC-033 | Inactive product behavior | May inactive or archived products appear on new purchases and sales? | Historical products must remain for history without cluttering operations. | Inactive products cannot be added to new purchase or sales documents. They remain visible in history, inventory remaining stock, and reports. Archived products are hidden from operational search. | Allow sales of inactive; hard-hide immediately. | Affects catalog, operations, and reporting. |
| DEC-034 | Concurrent competing sales | When two users sell the last available units, what is the business outcome? | This is a core inventory integrity expectation. | Only one confirmation may succeed for a given available quantity. The other user must be told stock is no longer available. No silent oversell. | Oversell with warning; first-draft wins. | Affects sales, inventory integrity, and user experience. |
| DEC-035 | AI recommendation acceptance process | Does accepting an AI recommendation immediately create an operational document, or only create a draft? | Acceptance is an operational control point. | Acceptance creates a draft in the normal application workflow and still requires the usual business approvals before execution. | Acceptance immediately submits/executes; acceptance only bookmarks advice. | Affects AI actions, approvals, and audit. |
